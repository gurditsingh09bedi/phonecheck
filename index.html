Kaun sa app aapka camera aur mic chupke se use karta hai?

Aapka iPhone har app ka camera, microphone, location, contacts aur photos use karna khud record karta hai, aur ye bhi ki app kin websites se baat karta hai. Ye page wo record padh ke aasaan report banata hai. File pehle se hai? Seedha upload par jao.

  1. Settings → Privacy & Security → App Privacy Report kholo aur Turn On App Privacy Report dabao.
  2. Phone normal use karo. Report pichhle 7 din ka record rakhti hai. Pehli baar ON kiya hai to kam se kam 1 din ruko.
  3. Usi screen par sabse neeche jao aur Save App Activity dabao (purane iOS me upar right ka share button). Phir Save to Files.
  4. Yahin wapas aao, Choose file dabao aur wo file chuno (naam: App_Privacy_Report_….ndjson).

    iPhone safety checklist

    iPhone par spying aksar kisi app se nahi, balki in settings se hoti hai. Har ek kholo aur tick karo. Aapke ticks sirf is browser me yaad rehte hain.

    Real time me kaise pakdein

    iOS kisi bhi app ya website ko doosre apps par real time nazar rakhne nahi deta, isliye iPhone par aisa monitor app ban hi nahi sakta. Apple ka apna system hi real-time alarm hai, aur use koi app band nahi kar sakta:

    Green dot

    Screen ke upar right me green dot = camera abhi chal raha hai (mic bhi saath ho sakta hai).

    Orange dot

    Orange dot = sirf microphone chal raha hai. Bina call/voice note ke dikhe to dhyan do.

    Control Center

    Dot dikhte hi upar right se neeche swipe karo. Sabse upar us app ka naam likha aata hai jisne abhi camera/mic use kiya.

    Har din dekho

    App Privacy Report ON rakho. Shak ho to apna time likh lo aur baad me file yahan dal ke us time ka record dekho.

    Normal cases: Siri / "Hey Siri" mic sunta hai, par Apple usko dot me nahi dikhata. Keyboard ka dictation, Face ID aur WhatsApp/FaceTime calls bhi aate hain. Shak ki baat ye hai: koi app jise camera ki zaroorat hi nahi, raat me use, ya bina aapke khole lambe time tak mic chalna.

    Ye tool aapki file sirf aapke browser me padhta hai, kuch upload nahi karta. Ye antivirus nahi hai, sirf aapke iPhone ke apne record ko aasaan bhasha me dikhata hai. Gambhir shak ho to iOS update karo, Apple ID ka password badlo, aur Apple Support se sampark karo.
